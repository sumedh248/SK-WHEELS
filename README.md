# 🚗 SK Wheels – Car Workshop Website

SK Wheels is a modern and responsive car workshop website designed to showcase automotive services, car repair solutions, and vehicle maintenance. The website provides customers with an easy way to explore available services and learn more about the workshop.

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 **Home Page** – Attractive landing page with workshop information.
- 🔧 **Automotive Services** – Showcase car servicing, repairs, and maintenance.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Designed to work across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- 🏢 **About Us** – Information about the workshop and its services.
- 📞 **Contact Section** – Provides contact information for customer inquiries.
- 🎨 **Modern UI** – Clean and user-friendly interface.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of SK Wheels is to establish an online presence for a car workshop through a professional website that presents its services, improves customer accessibility, and makes workshop information easier to find.
